Milestones

Our Entrepreneurial Journey

From bedroom sketches to launching an official brand, custom studio drops, and inspiring young creators.

Phase 01 • The Spark Early Discovery
💡

Falling in Love With Nail Art & Studio Craft

Weekend experiments with vibrant polishes quickly revealed something bigger: nails are wearable mini-canvases of personality. Ayesha began studying precision line art, while Abdullah saw the potential to build a brand that empowers youth creativity.

🎨 50+ Prototype Designs ⭐ Gel Curing Mastery
Phase 02 • First Drops First 20 Custom Sets
💅

Handcrafted Sets for Friends & Family

We launched our initial collection for classmates and family members. The reaction was electric: clients loved the snug custom fit, the 3D charms, and the chip-resistant durability. That confirmed our vision: this was a viable business.

📦 100% Five-Star Reviews ✨ First Paying Customers
Phase 03 • Brand Rebirth Brand Identity Launched
🌟

NOVA NAILS is Born: Dream, Create, Shine

We established NOVA NAILS with our official motto: Dream, Create, Shine. Abdullah structured our administrative operations, while Ayesha developed our signature pop aesthetic and packaging kits.

🚀 Official Logo & Brand Launch 📸 Instagram Growth
Phase 04 • Studio Growth Studio Scaling
🎨

Bespoke Collections & Creative Masterclasses

We scaled up from small batches to bespoke customized drops. Abdullah streamlined order logistics and customer support, while Ayesha hosted live design masterclasses teaching fellow young creators the secrets of 3D charms and color harmonies!

🛍️ Custom Orders Scaling 💡 Inspiring Next-Gen Founders
Phase 05 • What's Next Expanding Horizons
🚀

DIY Starter Kits & Nationwide Pop-Up Studios

Our roadmap includes launching all-in-one DIY press-on design kits for kids and teens, expanding into retail pop-up shops, and establishing a youth creator incubator to mentor ambitious young founders.

🛍️ DIY Kits in Development 🌐 Nationwide Pop-Ups
